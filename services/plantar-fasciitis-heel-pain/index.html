Plantar Fasciitis & Heel Pain

Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of heel pain. It results from inflammation of the plantar fascia, a thick band of tissue connecting the heel to the toes. Treatment includes stretching, orthotics and other therapies to reduce pain and restore mobility.

Schedule an appointment

Signs you should see a podiatrist

  • Sharp heel pain with your first steps in the morning
  • Pain after standing or sitting for a long time
  • Tenderness on the bottom of the heel
  • Heel pain that flares after exercise

How we help

  • A thorough exam to confirm the cause of your heel pain
  • Stretching and home-care programs
  • Supportive footwear advice and custom orthotics
  • Other conservative therapies to calm inflammation

This page is general information, not medical advice. Please schedule a visit for a diagnosis and a treatment plan that's right for you.

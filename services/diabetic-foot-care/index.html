Diabetic Foot Care

People with diabetes need specialized foot care to prevent ulcers, infections and circulation problems. Regular checkups and proper footwear help keep your feet healthy and reduce complications.

Schedule an appointment

Signs you should see a podiatrist

  • Numbness, tingling or burning in the feet
  • Cuts or blisters that heal slowly
  • Changes in skin color or temperature
  • Thick nails or calluses that are hard to care for at home

How we help

  • Routine diabetic foot exams
  • Safe nail and callus care
  • Footwear and diabetic shoe guidance
  • Education on daily foot checks and early warning signs

This page is general information, not medical advice. Please schedule a visit for a diagnosis and a treatment plan that's right for you.
